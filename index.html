Super Cat

A web browser platformer created by Joseph

v04 · 7 worlds · 6 stages each · 4 save files · created by Joseph

How to play

Move with ← → or A D. Jump with ↑, W, or Space. On a phone, use the buttons.

Tap jump for a little hop. Hold jump for a bigger hop. Press jump again in the air for a double jump. Tap the second jump for a small boost, or hold it for a bigger one.

Collect fish. Every 50 fish is a 1-up. Regular stages end at the flag. Castle bosses drop a colored gem — touch the gem to finish the world. World 7 ends when you beat Teacup. No gem there.

You have 3 hearts. Enemies and spikes take one heart. If all three hearts are gone, you lose a life. Falling in a hole costs a whole life right away. A new life always comes back with 3 hearts.

Press P or ESC to open Joseph's Workshop and mess with the game while you play.
